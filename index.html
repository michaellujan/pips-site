Pip's Midnight Heist

A cozy, one-thumb sneaking puzzle about a tiny mouse, a birthday cake and some very sleepy cats. For iPhone and iPad.

Privacy policySupport

How it plays

Grandma's birthday is tomorrow, and the cake is still missing its ingredients. After dark, Pip tiptoes through the house to fetch them. Swipe to move one tile; then every cat takes its turn. Slip past their lamplit gaze, wait for the right moment, and make it home with the cream, the sprinkles and the ribbon. Play at your own pace: there are no timers, and no energy to run out.

Collect stars, decorate Pip's burrow, dress Pip up, make friends with the cats, and come back each night for a new Daily Heist.
